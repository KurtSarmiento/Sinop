Sinop Brandbook
Brand guidelines · v1.0

Sinop

Digital Library and Reading Management System. Sinop helps readers find, borrow, reserve and return books, and helps librarians keep the catalog and circulation in order. The brand should feel like a well-kept reading room: warm, orderly and quietly confident.

01 · Logo

An open book with a bookmark ribbon

The open book stands for the catalog. The gold ribbon marks your place, which is what the reading list and reservations do. Use the full lockup first, and the icon alone where space is tight (app icon, favicon).

Clear space

Keep a margin equal to the width of the ribbon (one quarter of the icon) on all sides.

Minimum size

Icon: 24 px. Full lockup: 120 px wide. Below that, use the icon only.

Don't

Stretch, recolor outside the palette, add shadows, or place the maroon icon on a maroon background.

02 · Color

Maroon, gold and paper

Rough balance: 60% Paper, 30% Maroon, 10% Gold. Gold is for accents and highlights, never for long text.

Binding Maroon#6D1A2BPrimary. Headings, buttons, navigation.
Gilt Gold#C8A038Accent. Ribbon, highlights, reservations.
Paper#F7F1E5Background. Pages, cards, forms.
Deep Maroon#3F0F19Support. Dark mode, text on gold.
Ink#2A1015Support. Body text on Paper.

04 · Design principles

How Sinop should look and feel

Calm and ordered

Generous space, clear grids, one primary action per screen. A library is quiet.

Books come first

Covers and titles lead. Interface chrome stays Paper and Maroon so covers carry the color.

Gold means "yours"

Use gold for what belongs to the reader: reservations, saved books, reading list marks.

Clear status

Available, reserved and overdue always use the same pill style, on web and in the Kotlin app.
